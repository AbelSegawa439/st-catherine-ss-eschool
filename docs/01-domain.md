# Domain — Uganda secondary (MoES / NCDC / UNEB)

## Classes
S1–S4 = UCE. S5–S6 = UACE.
S1–S2: 11 compulsory + 1 elective.
S3–S4: 7 compulsory + 1–2 electives.
S5–S6: 3 principals + General Paper + Sub Maths or ICT.

## S1–S2 compulsory
English, Mathematics, Entrepreneurship, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History and Political Education, Kiswahili, Physical Education, Religious Education.

## S3–S4 compulsory
English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History and Political Education.

## UCE grades (CBC)
A Exceptional · B Outstanding · C Satisfactory · D Basic · E Elementary  
20% school CA + 80% UNEB exam. No divisions.

Student: LIN, class, stream, day/boarding, parents.
